Iron Oasis Gym - Billing, Training & Facility Guide 2026

1. Membership Freeze Policy
Members may pause an active membership for up to 60 days per calendar year due to travel or medical leave. A $10 per month administrative freeze fee applies. Submit freeze requests at least 7 days before your next billing date through the member portal.

2. Personal Training Packages
Certified personal training is available in two bundle tiers. The Starter 4-pack costs $280 and expires after 60 days. The Pro 12-pack costs $780 and expires after 180 days. Session cancellations require at least 24 hours advance notice to avoid forfeiting the session credit.

3. Guest Pass Rules
Platinum members receive 2 complimentary guest passes every calendar month. Standard members may purchase a single-day guest pass for $20. All visitors must present valid government photo ID at reception and sign the digital liability waiver before entering the floor.

4. Parking Validation
Members receive 3 hours of free validated parking in the North Garage adjacent to the main entrance. Scan your parking ticket barcode at the reception validation kiosk before exiting the club. Unvalidated parking is billed at standard garage rates.

5. Age & Youth Policy
Independent gym access requires members to be at least 16 years old. Youth ages 14 to 15 may train on the main floor only when directly accompanied by a parent or legal guardian at all times. Children under 14 are not permitted in free-weight areas.

6. Locker Rentals
Day-use lockers are free to all members during their visit when secured with a personal padlock. Permanent overnight locker rentals are available for $15 per month and include built-in combination locks and laundry drop service.
