Iron Oasis Gym - Member Handbook 2026

Welcome to the Iron Oasis! This document contains our official policies, specialized class schedules, and specific rules that apply to all members.

1. General Club Rules

  • Towel Policy: All members MUST bring a sweat towel. No towel, no workout. Blue towels can be rented for $2 at the front desk.
  • Dress Code: Closed-toe athletic shoes only. Jeans or work boots are strictly prohibited on the gym floor.
  • Quiet Hours: Between 2:00 PM and 4:00 PM daily, we observe "Low Impact Hours" where heavy deadlifting and loud music are restricted.

2. Membership & Cancellations

Cancellation Secret: To cancel a membership without the $50 early exit fee, members must provide a written notice at least 29 days in advance. If you move more than 15 miles away, the fee is waived (proof of address required).

4. Hidden Perks

All Platinum members have access to the "Oasis Secret Sauna" located behind the juice bar. Use the keypad code 8822# to enter.

5. Lost & Found

Items found on the floor are kept for 14 days. Valuables (phones, jewelry) are kept in the manager's safe for 30 days. After 30 days, unclaimed items are donated to the "Weights for Wellness" charity.
